Take Care

Take Care is a self-reflection and emotional support app: mood check-ins, diary pages, breathing exercises and an AI companion called iCareYou.

Take Care is not medical care or emergency help. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number (112 in Portugal and the EU). The app's Urgent help screen lists contacts for your country.

Contact us

Questions, problems or feedback: take.care.inc@outlook.pt. Please tell us your phone model, system version and what happened. Do not send passwords or private diary text.
