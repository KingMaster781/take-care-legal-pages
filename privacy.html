Privacy Policy

Take Care · Last updated: 18 November 2026

Take Care is a self-reflection and emotional support app. Your story stays yours: this page explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and the choices you have. It is written in plain language on purpose.

Take Care is not medical care. The AI companion "iCareYou" is an AI, not a therapist. It can make mistakes, does not diagnose, and does not replace professional help. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in Portugal and the EU).

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Take Care. Contact: take.care.inc@outlook.pt. [LEGAL NAME AND POSTAL ADDRESS: add if you are or become a company or trader]

What we collect

  • Account: email address, your preferred name or username, pronouns (optional), profile photo (optional), and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider). If you sign in with Google, Apple or Facebook, we receive your name and email from that service, never your password.
  • What you write: mood check-ins (mood, topics, notes), personal diary pages, and your conversations with iCareYou.
  • iCareYou's memory (optional, off by default): if you turn it on, iCareYou can keep a few short notes from your chats that help it support you later, for example the name of someone close to you or what helps you. It is told not to keep anything said in a crisis, health information, or details about sexuality, religion, politics, money, abuse or trauma, but it can make mistakes. You can read every note under Profile → What iCareYou remembers, delete any of them, or turn memory off at any time.
  • Preferences: conversation style, reminder settings, theme, language, and your device time zone.

We do not use advertising trackers, do not sell your data, and do not build advertising profiles.

Why we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, save and show your check-ins, diary pages and chats.
  • To show your insights (for example, your mood over a week).
  • To let iCareYou reply to you. By default, iCareYou only sees what you type in the chat. Your check-ins are used only if you turn on "Use check-ins in chat" and mark them as shared; diary pages are used only if you choose "Share with iCareYou" for that page. You can change these choices at any time.
  • To send reminders you asked for. Reminders are scheduled on your own device.

Our legal bases under the GDPR are:

  • Performing the service you asked for (Article 6(1)(b), contract) for your account and the basic running of the app.
  • Your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)) for health data: your moods, check-ins, diary pages, chats and memory notes can reveal information about your mental health, which is a special category of data. We process it only because you choose to record and share it. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning sharing off or deleting your data; withdrawing does not affect what was done before.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) for basic security, such as limiting abuse of the sign-up form.

Record of your consent

When you create an account we store that you accepted the Terms and this Privacy Policy, when you accepted, which version of the documents you accepted, and that you confirmed you are old enough to use the app. We do not store your date of birth. When these documents change in a way that matters, we ask you to accept the new version.

Technical data

To protect the sign-up form from abuse, the username availability check keeps a rate-limit record that includes the IP address of the device making the request, for about one hour. Our providers (Supabase and Google) also keep ordinary technical logs of requests, such as IP address, time and error information, for security and to keep the service working. We do not use this data for advertising. We also keep usage counters (for example, how many messages you sent today, to apply the daily limit) and the record of your consent described above, for as long as your account exists.

On your phone

A copy of your recent data is kept on your phone, encrypted, so the app works offline. It is wiped when you sign out. Your sign-in session is kept in the phone's secure storage. The optional app lock (Face ID, fingerprint or PIN) never leaves your phone. Reminders are scheduled on your phone.

Feedback and reports

Send feedback opens your email app with the app version, platform and system version filled in. We only receive what you send.

Report this reply stores the AI reply you reported (which we can read), the time and your account id, so we can review it. We keep reports for 12 months.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and file storage. Our project is hosted in Europe (London, United Kingdom; AWS region eu-west-2).
  • Google (Gemini) generates iCareYou's replies. When you send a message, the text of the message, any check-ins or diary pages you chose to share, and the memory notes if you turned memory on, are sent to Google for that purpose only.
    How Google may use it: We use Google's paid Gemini API service, under which Google does not use your messages to train or improve its models. Google may keep requests for a limited time to monitor abuse, under its Gemini API terms. [CHECK Google's current Gemini API terms and add the exact period].
  • Sentry helps us find crashes. A crash report contains technical details (the error, app version, phone model and system version) and an anonymous id. It never contains what you write, screenshots or your email. Reports are stored in [SENTRY REGION].
  • Expo provides the update service that delivers fixes without a new download. The request includes the app version, platform and IP address, and no personal content.
  • Google, Apple and Facebook process your sign-in if you use one of them, under their own privacy policies.

Some of these providers process data outside the European Economic Area: Supabase (London, United Kingdom), Google Gemini (processing may take place outside the EEA), Sentry ([SENTRY REGION]) and Expo (United States). Where required we rely on safeguards such as the EU's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete something, it is removed. When you delete your account, your profile, check-ins, diary pages, chats, memory notes, preferences, consents, reports, usage counters, photo and encryption key are permanently deleted. Backups are overwritten within [N] days [your Supabase plan's backup retention]. Deleting your account also destroys your encryption key, so older backups cannot be read.

Your rights and controls

Inside the app, under Profile → Privacy & data, you can export a copy of your data, delete all chats, all check-ins or all diary pages, delete individual memory notes (or turn memory off), and delete your account. Under the GDPR you also have the right to access, correct, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD, cnpd.pt) or your local authority. To use a right the app does not cover, email us.

See also: how to delete your data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by per-user access rules, so each account can read only its own data. Your diaries, notes and chats are stored encrypted, with a key for your account. We do not read them, except a reply you choose to report. What you choose to share with iCareYou is sent to Google's Gemini to produce a reply. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Children

Take Care is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
