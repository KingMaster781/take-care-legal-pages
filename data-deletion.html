Delete your data

Take Care · Last updated: 16 November 2026

You can delete your Take Care data at any time. This also covers accounts created with Google, Apple or Facebook.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Take Care and go to Profile.
  2. Open Privacy & data.
  3. Choose what to remove: Delete chats, Delete check-ins, Delete diaries, or Delete account to remove everything.
  4. Confirm. Deleting the account permanently removes your profile, photo, check-ins, diary pages, chats, iCareYou memory notes and preferences.

To remove only what iCareYou remembers, open Profile → What iCareYou remembers: delete single notes, or turn memory off.

Tip: use Export chats & check-ins first if you want to keep a copy.

By email

If you can't open the app, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Take Care data". We will delete your account and data and confirm by email, normally within 30 days.

Signed in with Facebook, Google or Apple

Deleting your Take Care account removes the data we hold. To also stop the connection with Facebook, go to Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites and remove "Take Care". For Google, use Google Account → Security → Third-party access; for Apple, Settings → Apple ID → Sign in with Apple.
